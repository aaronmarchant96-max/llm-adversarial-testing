## 🎯 The Core Loop

```
Messy Input
    ↓
Find the pressure point
    ↓
Separate surface signal from real structure
    ↓
Produce a reviewable output
    ↓
Keep human judgment and limits visible
```

## 🧠 The 8-Step Framework

### 1. **C**ollect
**Goal:** Gather all relevant information, data, and context
- No stone unturned
- Multiple sources, perspectives, and angles
- Raw data before interpretation

**Example:** For genealogy research, collect all primary sources, family records, and existing compilations before forming conclusions.

### 2. **A**nalyze
**Goal:** Examine patterns, identify limits, separate fact from interpretation
- Look for structural weak points
- Identify what breaks under pressure
- Find the hinge (decision point)

**Example:** In Debate Furnace, identify whether the disagreement is about facts, values, definitions, or trust.

### 3. **R**ecord
**Goal:** Document with full citations and source trails
- Every claim has a source
- Every decision has a rationale
- Audit trail is mandatory

**Example:** Story Forge keeps the source pattern visible in every generated blueprint.

### 4. **D**istinguish
**Goal:** Keep evidence separate from inference, facts from stories
- Evidence = verifiable data
- Inference = interpretation of data
- Stories = narrative construction

**Example:** Storm Replay separates signal detection from event classification.

### 5. **O**rganize
**Goal:** Structure information for human review and action
- Clear hierarchies
- Logical groupings
- Actionable outputs

**Example:** CARDO GUARD organizes costs, probabilities, and recommendations into a decision gate.

### 6. **R**eview
**Goal:** Validate against known truths, check for gaps and conflicts
- Cross-reference with established facts
- Identify contradictions
- Flag uncertainties

**Example:** Genealogy research flags "Needs Review" for unverified records.

### 7. **E**valuate
**Goal:** Assign confidence levels, make cost-weighted decisions
- Confidence tiers (Primary Source, Strong Evidence, Needs Review, etc.)
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Risk assessment

**Example:** CARDO GUARD calculates expected action waste vs. expected miss loss.

### 8. **I**terate
**Goal:** Refine based on feedback and new evidence
- Incorporate lessons learned
- Adjust thresholds and parameters
- Improve the system

**Example:** Tracepoint calibration passes refine detection thresholds.

## 🎯 The Hinge Concept

**Definition:** The point where a reasonable person could switch sides if their values, definitions, evidence threshold, risk tolerance, or trust assumptions changed.

**Questions to Find the Hinge:**
- What is each side protecting?
- What does each side fear losing?
- Is the disagreement about facts, definitions, values, trust, identity, policy mechanics, or risk?
- What evidence or principle would actually change the verdict?
- What can both sides admit without surrendering their position?

**Example (Debate Furnace):**
- Hinge: "whether free will requires ultimate authorship, or whether reason-responsive agency is enough"

**Example (CARDO GUARD):**
- Hinge: "the breakeven point where acting becomes more expensive than ignoring"
